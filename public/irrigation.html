Avocado Irrigation Management

Proper irrigation is important for healthy avocado trees, especially during dry periods. GreenHarvest helps farmers plan and manage their irrigation activities.

💧 Drip Irrigation

Deliver water directly to the root zone of avocado trees while reducing water wastage.

🚿 Sprinkler Irrigation

Use sprinkler systems to distribute water across the farm where appropriate.

🌧️ Rainwater Management

Plan how to collect and use available rainwater for avocado farming.

📊 Irrigation Scheduling

Develop an irrigation schedule based on farm conditions, tree age and weather conditions.

Irrigation Tips for Avocado Farmers

💧 Monitor Soil Moisture

Check soil moisture regularly to avoid both water stress and excessive watering.

🌱 Consider Tree Age

Young avocado trees generally require different irrigation management from mature trees.

☀️ Monitor Dry Periods

Adjust irrigation schedules during prolonged dry periods to protect your avocado trees.

🌧️ Use Rainfall Wisely

Take advantage of rainfall and avoid unnecessary irrigation when sufficient natural moisture is available.
