Pest & Disease Diagnosis

Report avocado pests and diseases and receive professional agricultural guidance.

Common Avocado Problems

Root Rot

A serious avocado disease that can cause yellowing leaves, poor growth and eventual tree decline.

Anthracnose

A fungal disease that can affect avocado leaves, flowers and fruits.

Avocado Thrips

Small insects that can damage young leaves and fruit, reducing fruit quality.

Scale Insects

Insects that attach to plant surfaces and may weaken avocado trees.

Fruit Damage

Fruit may develop spots, lesions or other damage caused by pests, diseases or environmental conditions.

Leaf Problems

Yellowing, curling, spots or unusual leaf growth may indicate nutrient, pest or disease problems.

Submit a Disease Diagnosis Request

Upload a clear image of the affected leaves, fruits, branches or other affected parts of the tree.

What Happens After Submission?

1. Request Received

Your disease or pest report is received by the farm management system.

2. Agronomist Review

An agronomist reviews the symptoms and any images submitted.

3. Diagnosis & Advice

You receive recommendations for managing the identified pest, disease or farm problem.

4. Follow-Up

Further farm visits or treatment recommendations can be arranged when necessary.
