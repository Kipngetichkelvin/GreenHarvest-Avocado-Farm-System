How the Avocado Market Works

1. Farmer Lists Produce

Farmers provide information about their avocado variety, quantity, quality and expected price.

2. Buyers Browse

Buyers can view available avocado produce and contact farmers.

3. Order is Placed

The buyer selects the desired produce and places an order.

4. Delivery

The produce is prepared and delivered to the agreed destination.
